Five-family word solver (word_solver.py): what was measured
===========================================================

What it does: solves five families directly from the wording and shows its reading.
    sum_difference   "sum 90 and difference 14", "one is 8 bigger than the other", a board cut in two, two ages
    consecutive      "the sum of 3 consecutive odd integers is 87", "three integers in a row total 138"
    work_together    two workers' separate times -> time together (1/a + 1/b)
    percent_change   the price BEFORE a percent rise (raise, markup, tax, tip) or fall (discount, off)
    proportion       "If 3 kg of apples cost $7.50, how much do 8 kg cost?"  (three numbers, direct proportion)
Safety rules (the same idea as number_puzzle.py): it answers only if the wording pattern matches, EVERY number in
the problem has a role, and the answer passes a sanity check.  Anything else -> None and the matcher takes over.

Tests (python test_word_solver.py): 9 tests, all pass
  - phrasings per family and problems it must decline (a missing role, a saving instead of a price, a negative
    number, parity that does not fit, inverse proportion, workers who drain or leak ...)
  - ground truth, all 1,000 generated problems: claimed 74 (sum_difference 13, consecutive 5, work_together 5,
    percent_change 22, proportion 29), wrong 0.  Every claim is in the intended categories.
        NOTE: written while looking at these problems, so this is a development result.
  - the 100 hand-written matcher problems: it claims 13, and all 13 match answers I calculated by hand.
  - 33 FRESH wordings (fresh_word_problems.py) written after the solver was finished and run once, untuned:
        21 problems with an answer: 18 solved, 3 declined (safe), 0 wrong.   12 required declines: 12 of 12 declined.
        The 3 gaps: "...12% service charge..." (charge is not in the rise words), "A machine makes 240 bolts in 8 hours.
        How many in 20 hours?" ('make' is not a cue word), "...how far does it go in 5 hours?" ('how far' is not matched).
        One item in the fresh file was mislabelled by me (a leftover expression that expected a decline for a problem
        whose true answer is 24/5); the solver's 4.8 was right.  I corrected the label, not the solver.

Bugs the tests found (each has a regression test now)
  1. proportion was far too loose (52 claims, 28 wrong on the generated problems: overtime pay, speed of sound, estates).
     Fixed by roles by position (the known pair BEFORE the question, the asked quantity AFTER it) and by rejecting
     rates written with / or =, "first", "after that", "split", "together", "per minute".
  2. work_together answered a problem that gives the TOGETHER time and asks for one worker alone (3.75 h, truth 15 h).
     Found by hand-checking set B.  Fixed by declining anything that mentions "alone", "the other" or "each other".
  3. work_together answered "a tap fills a bath in 10 min but the plug leaks and empties it in 30 min" (7.5, truth 15).
     The leak check used word boundaries and missed "leaks" and "empties".  Found by the hand-set test.
  4. percent_change could answer "A shirt costs $40 and is 25% off. What is the sale price?" with the wrong value, and
     "25% off, saving $10. What was the original price?" (the $10 is a saving).  Fixed: the question sentence must ask
     for the value BEFORE the change (before / original / old / wholesale / itself ...), and amounts that follow
     "by $", "of $", "saving" are rejected.

Effect on the matcher's 100 hand-written problems (number reader, then the five families, then the matcher):
                                plain matcher       + number reader      + reader + five families
  set A (60, tuned on)          48/60  58/60        49/60  58/60         51/60  60/60        (top-1 / top-3)
  set B (40, never tuned)       29/40  36/40        31/40  37/40         34/40  40/40
  set B top-1: 72.5% -> 77.5% -> 85.0%    top-3: 90.0% -> 92.5% -> 100%
  The problems it answers are answered exactly, not only given a type.

What this does NOT show
  * The families were chosen BECAUSE of the matcher's misses in sets A and B, so those sets are no longer a clean
    test for these five families.  The 33 fresh wordings (18 of 21 solved, 0 wrong) are the honest check.
  * Coverage is small: the solver claims 74 of the 1,000 generated problems (7.4%); with the number reader, 102 (10%).
    The other types still depend on the matcher alone.
  * All wordings (generated, hand-written, fresh) come from the same author as the solver.

Batch 2: three cue gaps (service charge, "how far", the noun "machine")
=======================================================================
Changes (word_solver.py only):
  1. _UP gets the phrase "service charge" (not the bare word "charge").
  2. the question pattern gets "far":  how (much|many|long|far)
  3. "machines?" removed from _REJECT_PROP (workers, men, pipes, taps, pumps stay).
  Also earlier today (Grok's edit and the together-word rule): answers stay exact fractions; a work problem whose
  setup already says "together" is declined.

Required cases (all now in test_word_solver.py):
  "...12% service charge was $224. What was the bill before the charge?"                  -> 200
  "A bill of $90 includes a $10 service charge. What was it before the service charge?"   -> declines (no % number)
  "A shirt is 25% off, saving $10. What was the original price?"                           -> declines
  "A train travels 150 km in 2 hours. At that rate, how far does it go in 5 hours?"         -> 375
  "A car goes 60 mph for 3 hours then 40 mph for 2 hours. How far does it go in all?"       -> declines
  "A machine makes 240 bolts in 8 hours. How many bolts does it make in 20 hours?"         -> 600
  "If 6 workers build a wall in 12 days, how long would 9 workers take?"                   -> declines
  (The bolts problem was NOT a missing cue: "makes" was already a cue; the noun "machine" was in the reject list.)

      spring problems ("Stretch varies directly with force. How far will it stretch under ... N?"), e.g. 2.5 cm under
      20 N -> 5 cm, 6 cm under 12 N -> 45/2 (stored 22.5 cm); every answer equals the stored answer.
      The service-charge and machine wordings do not occur in the generated set, so they added no claims there.
  first fresh set (33):  now a DEVELOPMENT set (it found these gaps): all 21 with an answer solved, all 12 required
      declines still decline.
  SECOND fresh set (fresh_word_problems_2.py, 27 wordings written after the edits, run once, untuned):

      The 3 declined are new gaps, all missing cue words for the rate family:
          "A bus covers 210 miles in 6 hours. How far does it go in 9 hours?"       ("covers")

          "A printer prints 48 pages in 3 minutes. How many ... in 10 minutes?"     ("prints")
      They were NOT fixed after the run.
  The stored answer stays the exact fraction; the reading carries the nearest-cent text for money.
