Let: x = cartons of 12, y = cartons of 30
Equation: x + y = 41;  12x + 30y = 924
Solve: Solve by elimination → x = 17, y = 24
Answer: 17 of 12, 24 of 30

#0950 [System of equations (3 variables)]
Problem: An apple, a banana, and a cherry cup cost $4.05 together. Two apples and a banana cost $3.40. A banana and two cherry cups cost $4.70. Find each price.
Let: a, b, c = prices
Equation: a + b + c = 4.05;  2a + b = 3.4;  b + 2c = 4.7
Solve: Subtract eq 1 from eq 2: a − c = -0.65; substitute into eq 3 → a = 1.1, b = 1.2, c = 1.75
Answer: apple $1.10, banana $1.20, cherry cup $1.75

#0951 [System of equations (3 variables)]
Problem: Three numbers add up to 21. The first plus the second is 10, and the second plus the third is 12. Find all three.
Let: x, y, z = the numbers
Equation: x + y + z = 21;  x + y = 10;  y + z = 12
Solve: z = 21 − 10 = 11; x = 21 − 12 = 9; y = 10 − 9 = 1 → x = 9, y = 1, z = 11
Answer: 9, 1, 11
